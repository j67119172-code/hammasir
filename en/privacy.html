Privacy Policy

Pre-publication draft — October 25, 2026

Review this draft against the app’s actual practices and add a verified support contact before public launch.

Information processed

The service may process phone number, profile and vehicle details, city, commuting areas, selected origin and destination, working days, and travel time windows to provide matching functions.

Purposes

Information is used for account management, identifying compatible commutes, group proposals, notifications, and support requests.

Information shared with members

Member information should be disclosed according to group status and app permissions. Sensitive contact and precise addresses should only become available after group-formation requirements are satisfied.

Payments

If real payments are enabled, transaction identifiers and status may be processed for verification and support. Sensitive payment-card details must not be stored on HamMasir servers.

Security and retention

Data access must be restricted and protected. Final data retention, account deletion procedures, and infrastructure-provider disclosures must be confirmed before launch.

Contact

To request access, correction, or deletion, use the verified contact channel published on our support page.
